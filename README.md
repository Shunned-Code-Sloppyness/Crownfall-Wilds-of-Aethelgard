Crownfall: core concepts |Crownfall: Wilds of Aethelgard is a mobile-first and desktop-ready top-down medieval fantasy action-sandbox RPG inspired by Albion Online, built entirely in procedural code with zero external image or model assets. It pairs Albion's classless "you are what you wear" gear progression, multi-tier gathering, full player-driven crafting, banking, and homestead building with active dodge-rolls, timed parry counters, drag-aimed skill-shot combat, roaming world bosses, and a live hybrid server auction house shared with real players and autonomous AI adventurers.

User Review & Critical Decisions [!IMPORTANT] All core architectural and design choices below incorporate your Phase 1 selections and are ready for your final approval before implementation begins.

World Identity: Crownfall: Wilds of Aethelgard — a sunlit, hand-painted medieval frontier rendered 100% procedurally on an HTML5 2D Canvas with warm afternoon lighting, soft contact shadows, and rich biome particle systems.

Economy & Multiplayer Mode: Hybrid World with Shared Server Market & AI Adventurers — an authoritative Node/Express + WebSocket backend persists the shared Auction House, player-to-player direct trading, and world state across sessions while autonomous AI adventurers visibly roam zones, gather resources, fight creatures, list crafted goods on the market, and purchase player listings.

Control Scheme: Virtual Thumbstick + Drag Skill-Shots + Full Keyboard & Mouse — adaptive dual-input controls featuring a responsive left-hand virtual thumbstick and right-hand drag-to-aim skill-shot action wheel on touch devices, seamlessly paired with WASD movement, mouse-cursor aiming, Space dodge-roll, F parry counter, and Q/E/R skills on desktop. World Structure: Interconnected Zones from Safe Hub to High-Risk Boss Wilds — four seamless portal-linked regions escalating from Oakhaven Bastion (Safe Hub & Player Homestead) through Whispering Glade (Tier 1–2 Forest) and Sunstone Crags (Tier 3–4 Canyon) to Ashen Caldera (Tier 4–5 High-Risk Volcanic Boss Wilds).

Overview & Core Concept What It Does Players enter Aethelgard as an unbound adventurer whose combat role, attributes, and active abilities dynamically transform based on their equipped Weapon, Offhand, Helmet, Chest Armor, Boots, and Relic Artifact. Outside of combat, players gather tiered natural resources (gated by tool tier and boosted by gathering mastery), refine and craft every piece of equipment, potion, and furniture item via field campfires or town stations, furnish a personal Homestead Manor with functional storage chests and crafting benches, store valuables in the Town Vault, and trade directly with other players/AI adventurers or via the live Grand Auction Market. Target Audience & Play Context Designed for mobile touch players seeking tactile, skill-expressive action RPG combat without auto-play bloat, as well as desktop browser players who enjoy Albion Online's sandbox economy, risk-versus-reward zone progression, and build experimentation.

Key Value & Standout Additions Active Action Combat: Replaces passive stat-trading with manual Dodge-Rolls (invulnerability frames + wind ribbon trails), Parry Counters (timed guard stance that negates damage, triggers brief impact hitstop, stuns attackers, and unlocks an empowered Riposte strike), and Drag-Aimed Skill-Shots (linear projectiles, ground-targeted AoE rings, and cleave cones). Tactical Armor & Damage Matrix: Plate, Leather, and Cloth armors carry distinct physical/magic defense profiles, mobility modifiers, and active chest/helm skills, interacting against Slashing, Piercing, Crushing, and Arcane/Elemental damage types. 100% Procedural Painterly Engine: Every biome brushstroke, swaying tree canopy, shimmering mineral vein, layered paper-doll equipment sprite, boss telegraph, and particle burst is synthesized in real time at 60 FPS with zero external image or 3D model files.

User Experience & Visual Design Key User Flows Title Screen & Immediate Entry: Opens directly onto a live, full-viewport animated camera pan across Oakhaven Bastion and Whispering Glade bathed in warm golden-hour sunlight, drifting pollen motes, and roaming adventurers. Features a bespoke inline SVG crest lockup (Crownfall: Wilds of Aethelgard), a single primary Enter World CTA (Enter / Space / Tap), and one concise control-hint line. Active Exploration, Gathering & Combat: Move & Dodge: Left virtual thumbstick (touch) or WASD (desktop) moves smoothly; tapping Dodge (Space) triggers an invulnerable roll with dust/wind particles. Skill-Shot & Parry: Dragging any skill button (or moving mouse + pressing Q/W/R/LMB) projects a crisp trajectory line, cone, or AoE ring on the ground before firing; pressing Parry (F / RMB / Shield button) enters a 0.45s counter stance.

Tool-Gated Gathering: Approaching Timber, Ore, Fiber, Stone, or Hide nodes displays a contextual gather prompt and tier badge (T1–T5). If the equipped tool tier is too low, a clear warning toast and metallic spark feedback explain the required tool tier; valid gathering channels a progress arc with rhythmic strikes, particle showers, and mastery XP popups.

Streamlined Field Crafting & Town Stations: Field Crafting: Quick-craft campfires, Tier 1–2 tools, healing draughts, and field repair kits anywhere in the wild via a compact slide-over drawer. Town & Homestead Stations: Craft Tier 1–5 weapons, Plate/Leather/Cloth armor sets, Artifact gear (forged from World Boss relic cores), and household furniture.

Town Storage Bank & Homestead Manor: Town Bank: Deposit/withdraw gear, resources, and Silver into categorized vault tabs safe from high-risk zone drops. Homestead Manor: Enter your personal estate to place, rotate, and use crafted furniture—including Oak & Ironbound Storage Chests (expanding physical item storage), Master Workbenches, Alchemist Tables, Warm Hearths (granting rested regeneration buffs), Weapon Racks, and Tapestries.

Hybrid Auction Market & Direct Player/AI Trading: Grand Market: List items with custom Silver buyout prices, filter by gear slot/tier/rarity, buy listings from real players or AI adventurers, and collect Silver earnings. Direct Trade: Tap any nearby connected player or AI adventurer to open a two-sided barter window with item slots, Silver offer, and dual-lock confirmation.

Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs Decision 1: Custom Procedural 2D Canvas Engine vs. External Sprite/3D Assets Chosen Approach: A dedicated HTML5 2D Canvas renderer with offscreen procedural brush-texture caching, multi-layer paper-doll equipment drawing, dynamic afternoon directional shadows, and pooled particle systems. Why: Strictly fulfills the requirement for zero external image or model files while guaranteeing smooth 60 FPS on mobile browsers and instant load times with zero broken asset requests. Alternatives Considered: Three.js 3D primitives or static SVG DOM nodes; Three.js adds heavy mobile GPU overhead without hand-painted brushwork warmth, while DOM SVGs lag under hundreds of particles. Decision 2: Authoritative Node/WebSocket Hybrid Economy vs. Pure Client Storage

Chosen Approach: Express + WebSocket (ws) server managing a persistent server-side world & market state (JSON store) alongside client-side 60 FPS combat/movement prediction and autonomous AI adventurer agents. Why: Enables real-time shared Auction House listings, cross-tab/multi-user player presence, and direct player-to-player trading while keeping solo play lively through AI adventurers who gather, craft, roam, and trade in the same economy. Alternatives Considered: Pure localStorage simulation; rejected because it cannot support a true shared server market or real-time player-to-player trading. Decision 3: Decoupled Fixed-Timestep Simulation Ref vs. React State Loop

Chosen Approach: Run the 60 FPS requestAnimationFrame simulation (clamped dt <= 0.05s) inside an isolated engine controller ref, pushing throttled HUD snapshots (HP, Energy, Cooldowns, Nearby Prompts) to React UI overlays only when values change. Why: Prevents React reconciliation stalls during intense world boss fights and particle bursts, keeping mobile touch input latency under 16ms. Alternatives Considered: Storing entity coordinates in React state; rejected due to frame drops on mobile devices.

Classless Loadout & Destiny Mastery (PlayerProfile): Equipped slots (weapon, offhand, helmet, chest, boots, cape, tool), active skill bar derived dynamically from equipped Weapon (Basic, Q, W, R) + Chest/Helm passive/active perks, Silver balance, inventory bag, Town Bank vault, Homestead furniture layout (including chest inventories), and per-tree Mastery XP levels (Combat Trees + Gathering/Crafting Trees). World Zones, Nodes & Roaming Bosses (ZoneState): Four interconnected maps (Oakhaven Bastion, Whispering Glade, Sunstone Crags, Ashen Caldera) with risk rules (Safe, Knockdown, High-Risk), terrain obstacles, tiered resource nodes (Wood, Ore, Fiber, Hide, Stone), standard enemy camps, and patrolling Roaming World Bosses (Elder Sylvan Treant, Ironhide Warlord Krauss, Ignis, Cinder-Crowned Wyrm) with multi-stage telegraph states (patrol, windup, execute, staggered, respawning). Market & Direct Trade (MarketListing, TradeSession): Server-authoritative auction listings with seller ID (real player or AI adventurer), price, expiration, and item payload; real-time dual-party trade sessions supporting item & Silver escrow. Interactive Component & State Mapping Title & Game State Controller: Transitions cleanly between TITLE_SCREEN, PLAYING, PAUSED, DEFEATED (with zone-appropriate respawn & recovery summary), and BOSS_VICTORY celebration banners. Adaptive Combat & Gathering Controller: Processes held movement vectors + edge-triggered justPressed dodge-roll, parry counter, and drag-aimed skill-shots; validates tool tier against resource node tier before starting gather channels; computes damage multipliers across attacker damage type vs. defender armor class (Plate, Leather, Cloth). Crafting & Homestead Manager: Validates material & Silver costs for field crafting, town forge/loom/tanner/alchemist crafting, and house furniture crafting; manages interactive furniture placement inside the Homestead Manor and links placed Storage Chests to persistent storage grids. Market, Bank & Trade Sync Layer: Sends optimistic UI requests over WebSocket (market:list, market:buy, market:cancel, trade:offer, trade:confirm, bank:transfer) and reconciles authoritative server broadcasts idempotently.
